## Original game
Movement triggers: player move/queued move/move-with-facing/attack/formation-change; next queued order after current arrival; stance AGGRESSIVE=attack/chase visible enemy, CHICKEN=random escape ≤100m, NORMAL=shoot only. Computer squads: mission scripts; idle go-to/patrol/random-point/wander/follow; counterattack attacker of protected unit within last 2s; sideways step every 1s when unable to fire at target.
Routing: one squad route from member nearest destination; time-sliced weighted A* over obstacle-outline corners, 1024×1024 grid, 6.25m cells, heuristic weight 1.5, 60 expansions/tick, cap 500. Member k→slot k (reverse order on turnaround; no nearest-slot matching). Each member searches to route point 2 (budget 200), follows shared waypoints, drives final leg to slot without search. Arrival=all stopped OR 20s after all but one stop. Slot layout faces start→destination.
Tick: (1) skip to later visible waypoint/repath hidden current; (2) throttle=0 waiting/turning toward waypoint, 0.5 if waypoint behind, otherwise 1, reduced near slot to ≥0.6; (3) steer (`steer_to`: 1−cos⁶ of heading error, full lock beyond 90°) + AvoidUnits bias (drive/wait 2s/full-lock swerve); (4) turn at ω×steering, apply full friction/speed cap, integrate; (5) push out of units, slide on blocked cells, teleport off excessive slopes. Full friction nearly cancels acceleration, so throttle <~0.9 brakes. Movement runs once per rendered frame with measured frame time; fixed-step mode 0.04s.
Failure causes: 500 cap→"Movement denied" on long/complex routes; 60/tick→reaction lag. Queued moves, formation changes, attacks and all AI moves search all 500 within one tick→hitches. Coarse corner paths/zero-width edge rays (only corners have 2r clearance)→wall hugging/zigzags. `Mover::SetReachMid` 0x50ee40 ignores argument→intermediate reach=1 world unit; waypoint behind→half throttle→hard braking/circling. Nearest-destination leader + budget-200 joins + unsearched final legs→stragglers; arrival timeout leaves them. Successful repath→turn in place; terrain sliding flips passing side→stop/go. Moving friend deemed "will clear"→crossing/oncoming squads drive into each other, collision-push through.
Unit data: `ENTFILES/*.ENT` text in `Data0.sdf` (overrides `DATA1.sdf`, `DATA2.sdf`); `tools/sdf.py list|extract`; extracted copies `build/sdf/`.

## Key contracts
- Image: no relocations, fixed base 0x400000, MSVC6 `thiscall`.
- Grid: 1024×1024 cells, 2 bits each, at 0x6eb47c, 6.25 world units/cell; cell blocks mover when `value > moverClass`; value 2 (hills) blocks tanks (class 1) not infantry (class 2); 3 (cliffs) blocks everything; class 4 aircraft. `PathFinder::LoadGrid` (0x4e8200) is the only grid writer→static per map; stores file's last row as grid row 0.

## Formation following (form + coord)
- Avoidance (`avoidance.c`): ORCA (RVO2 velocity-obstacle method), nearest desired velocity collision-free over a short horizon; moving pairs share the effort, arrived or turning units are avoided fully, squad mates only near contact. Walls remain physics (ORCA wall constraints fight game routes that run along blocked cells); a new velocity is accepted only if the next moment is clear; units held still by avoidance bypass it for a while.
- Parked squads: `stamp_parked` stamps cells under parked units as cliffs during search; `is_in_sight` hides lines through parked units → routes go around them. Collision pushes the mover only (user: "parked units should not move", then "add back a slight motion"): `yield_parked` (`--yield off`) lets a threatened parked unit dodge a little and return (rare); an arrived member of a still-moving squad likewise yields to a mate.
- Route origin: game route starts at the member nearest the destination; recentered on the formation if the onward path is clear; shortened by skipping waypoints where a band as wide as the widest member is clear (removes corners and backtracking that swung every place around). `take_shorter_center_route` (route.c, `--centersearch off`; user: `pass` seed 93 squad "goes in a completely wrong direction at first"): second game search from the members' center, kept if clearly shorter. Cause: the game search adds corners around the first obstacle on the line to the destination → the start point decides which way around obstacles (seed 93: L-route, +67m).
- `smooth_route` (route.c, `--smooth off`; user wanted smooth curves, not kinked bumps): cubic B-spline whose control points are pushed away from blocked cells toward formation clearance → wide gentle detours, parallel lanes rather than obstacle-local bulges. Keeps the whole unsmoothed route if any sample lacks body room (few routes, tight cliff tips; heavies' `corridor` route always); partial blending did worse.
- `widen_curve` (route.c, `--wideturns off`; user: "avoid turns that force units to slow down before turning, favor wider turns"): second stage of `smooth_route`, after its pushes and only when the body clears; spreads bends sharper than the full-speed turn radius + formation half-width; best pass kept.
- Anchor (virtual leader on the route, `advance_anchor`, formation.c): slowest member top speed when together; slows with lag, crawls beyond a larger lag, stops beyond a very large one; ignores lag for a while after being held still (one stuck unit cannot hold forever). `--turnslow on` (off by default): also slows for sharp turns.
- Places: first match minimizes total squared distance (keeps the arrangement). `reassign_places` (`--reassign off`; user): periodic rematch in route coordinates (side weighted), only when clearly better. `--endstop` (on): one final match member→slot at the start of the end leg, then frozen (a new slot on the end leg made units stop and turn around). Most pairs keep left/right and front/back; plain distances do not keep order; a larger side weight worsens everything else.
- Lanes (`set_up_lanes`/`get_lane_point`, lanes.c; per-member lanes (user), `--lanes off`): slot side offsets fitted to the free width along the route, planned over the whole route (early obstacle response, handles cliff islands); lanes may merge, never cross (user; `sim/tools/check_lanes.py` verifies a trace). One shared route instead: far more wall scrapes than the game.
- Too narrow → single file down the passage center, front member first; gap grows with anchor speed (fixed gaps → bumps at 20+ m/s). Place = lane point at the anchor route distance + slot forward offset; blends from start positions at the route start and onto final slots at its end.
- Rejoin (unit far behind its place or without a straight path to it): drive straight at the furthest visible point of its own lane a little ahead, else of the squad route (adding anchor motion pushed units away). `is_rejoin_point_usable` (`--rejoinroom off`; user: `ridge` seed 99 stuck heavies): point must lie beyond the unit reach and be clear for its body. Causes: inside an unsmoothed sharp corner the clamped lane put all points under the heavy (spun, then stood); the reach test alone left a heavy held by the brake check at a hill cell (`ridge` seed 7).
- Lane pursuit (`--pursuit off`): a unit a few m off its place steers at its own lane ahead; the place sets only its speed. Steering straight at the place cut across bends and pointed units at walls (user saw units "change course too late and stop").
- Ahead of place (`slow_down_ahead`, `--aheadslow off`; user: a unit too far ahead stopped abruptly to wait): a unit ahead slows gently to a fraction of the anchor speed instead of a full-friction stop (lower floor in single file; slower drop-back ran units into mates merging in front). Most would-be stops were single-file entries (`winding`, `pass`).
- End leg (`--endstop off`; user: units stopped near their end point, then moved to it): on the last stretch of the route, a unit that sees its slot drives straight at it at braking speed, stops close to it, then turns to the ordered facing. Cause before: anchor still fast near the end, and slots face start→destination, so a slot could lie behind its unit → overshoot, stop, turn, drive back. `--endcap` (on; user: lineplain seed0 light squad "overshoots its destination, and slows down other squads that are waiting"): on the end leg ORCA may turn a unit but not speed it up past its end speed. Cause before: ORCA pushed braking front units on for mates coming up behind; they ended far past their slots and crept back.
- Steering (`steer_proportionally`, `--linearsteer off`→game `steer_to`): turn rate proportional to the heading error, closes it in ~0.1s for every kind. With `steer_to` (≈3×angle² for small angles) a medium tank in a bend trails its wanted direction ~20° and drifts outward onto walls. Final facing on the slot still uses `steer_to`.
- Bend speed (`limit_speed_for_bend`): speed limited by the arc from the heading to the own lane point ahead. `--bendlook` (on): that point lies further ahead (short chords braked units for their own side gap, not for bends; `winding` heavies most). Units brake while turning if the next moment along the heading hits blocked ground.
- Detours (`follow_detour`, replan.c, `--replan off`; user, after `ridge` seed 21 tank stuck for good behind a one-cell hill island): a unit that has not moved, is far from its place and has terrain on the line to its target searches with the game search to its own lane ahead, and follows that path until the line clears or a time limit. ## Rejected features
Built and tested, then removed or never made default; kept so they are not tried again blindly. One bullet per feature: name or switch (date if known; who removed it); what it did; why rejected. Results: 100 seeds, C, unless stated.

### Formation following
- Fast-kind anticipation `--fastlook`, `--fasthorizon` (removed (user)): light tanks (top speed / 20 m/s = 1.4) looked further ahead: `--fastlook` pursuit, bend and brake look times; `--fasthorizon` ORCA horizon. Why: neither helped; fastlook slower, far more bumps between squads; fasthorizon more stops and spin.
- `--regroup` (2026-11-15; kept by the user at 0.8, then removed with `--closeup` (user): "revert to the old formation switch, but make it smarter" → `--reformmatch`): on leaving single file, anchor kept ≥0.8 (`REGROUP_SPEED_FRACTION`) of speed for ≤10s instead of the lag-wait crawl. Why: replaced by the user; squad stayed strung out longer (spread grew with the fraction).
- `--closeup` (2026-11-15; removed with `--regroup` (user)): regroup ended at 2m lag instead of `LAG_WAIT_METERS` 8m; anchor kept the regroup fraction until then. Why: removed with `--regroup`; faster close-up cost arrival (squads closed up, then stretched out again at the next single file).
- `--dropback` (2026-11-15; removed (mine)): member ahead of its place kept enough speed to regain anchor speed as its place arrives. Why: more bumps within and between squads (it ran into mates merging in front).
- `--bodyway`, `--lanemotion` (2026-11-16; removed (user): "remove body way, lanemotion"; fixes for the pass seed 66 cliff hit, see Open): `--bodyway` pursuit and rejoin targets needed a clear body circle, not only a zero-width line; `--lanemotion` place moved with the lane change instead of the route direction. Why: bodyway worse on nearly every metric, unit still clipped the corner; lanemotion cleared the corner, but more stops, far more bumps within squads.
- Lane-tracking variants (see Evidence "Lane tracking"): anchor speed-up limit (slower: members follow anchor speed); places moving along own lane (no gain); bend limit also from lane curvature (slower, no fewer scrapes); pursuit target on lane tangent (reason not recorded: TODO: confirm); lane velocity of the place as feed-forward (no gain); end approach at braking speed (overshoot, much more spin); bend look 1.5/ω, bend margin 0.9 (smaller gains).
- Other formation variants: ORCA wall constraints (fight game routes along blocked cells); body-wide straight-line checks (more bumps); wall braking that keeps rolling at 20% (units grind along walls); heavies: curve keeping only the tightest clearance of the route (worse everywhere, pass squads stuck), 2–4m lag tolerance, lag thresholds scaled by radius (no gain); anchor cap from lane length per route meter (slower, one squad never arrived); bend limit from lane direction (far more wall scrapes and intrusion); bend margin 1.0 (a squad never arrived).
