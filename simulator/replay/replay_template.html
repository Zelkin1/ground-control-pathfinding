Squad Traffic Replay

Ground Control unit movement, replayed from the offline simulator: the path search and formation code of the game itself, its unit logic rebuilt from the decompile, and real unit values.

Base game: each squad follows one route, and at its end each member drives to its final parking position without a path search. Mod: an anchor moves along a curved route and waits for laggards. Each unit steers along its own lane, avoids the others with ORCA avoidance and stops on its final parking position. Each squad predicts the paths of the other moving squads: it re-routes around them, and two squads that meet head-on each move to their own side, or one squad slows down early so that it does not have to wait. The first "seed" tab is the worst of 100 runs with moved starts, and the others are runs that still need work. Simulated.
