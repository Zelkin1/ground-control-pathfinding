Squad Traffic Replay

Ground Control unit movement, replayed from the offline simulator: the path search and formation code of the game itself, its unit logic rebuilt from the decompile, and real unit values.

Base game: each squad follows one route; members drive the last leg to their slot. Form. + coord. + pass.: a leader point drives a smooth route and waits for laggards; each unit steers along its own lane, avoids the others and stops on its slot. Squads give way at crossings and narrow passes, move aside when they meet head-on, and a faster squad passes beside a slower one, which keeps its route. The first "seed" tab is the worst of 100 shifted runs, the others are runs still being fixed. Simulated.
